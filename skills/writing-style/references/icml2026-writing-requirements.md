# ICML 2026 writing-relevant requirements (quick summary)

This file lists the ICML 2026 constraints that most directly affect writing decisions.
For authoritative rules and deadlines, defer to the official ICML sources (call for papers / author kit / submission instructions).

## Main-text page budget

- Initial submission main text is capped (ICML-style: 8 pages). Plan writing around an 8-page outline.
- Appendix and references are outside the main-text limit, but should still be used responsibly.

## Required Impact statement

- ICML requires an Impact statement section (consult the official author instructions for exact placement rules).
- Writing tip: do not make it marketing. Be specific about intended use, plausible harms, and mitigations.

## Double-blind constraints

- Anonymous version should not include acknowledgements or identity-revealing URLs.
- Writing tip: avoid “in our previous work” phrasing; cite your prior work in third person.

## Supplementary material

- Writing tip: use appendix for proof/extra tables that support claims; use supplementary uploads for code/data/videos as needed.
- Do not rely on reviewers reading supplementary to understand your main claims.
