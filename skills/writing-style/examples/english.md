# English editing examples

These are expression exercises with supplied text, not factual sources about
real events. Each revision uses only information available in its input.
Use the user's own approved writing to calibrate the voice of an actual task.

## Example 1: Software update announcement

Before:

> The new software update serves as a testament to the company's commitment to innovation. Moreover, it provides a seamless, intuitive, and powerful user experience, ensuring that users can accomplish their goals efficiently. It's not just an update, it's a revolution in how we think about productivity. Industry experts believe this will have a lasting impact on the entire sector, highlighting the company's pivotal role in the evolving technological landscape.

After:

> The software update is intended to help users complete their work more efficiently.

The input supplies a general purpose and promotional claims. It supplies no
feature list, user study, or measured improvement. A more concrete announcement
needs those facts from the user or an authorized source; polishing cannot
supply them.

## Example 2: Travel description

Before:

> Nestled within the breathtaking region of Tuscany, the village stands as a vibrant testament to Italy's rich cultural heritage. Boasting a population of just 2,000 residents, it serves as a focal point for artists seeking inspiration from the rolling hills and cypress-lined roads that symbolize the enduring beauty of the Italian countryside.

After:

> The village is in Tuscany and has 2,000 residents. Artists visit for inspiration from the surrounding hills and cypress-lined roads.

The place, population, visitors, and landscape remain. Promotional evaluation
is removed without adding visitor activities or statistics.

## Example 3: Company profile

Before:

> Additionally, the company's commitment to fostering innovation is underscored by its extensive patent portfolio, which encompasses groundbreaking technologies ranging from quantum computing to sustainable energy solutions. This multifaceted approach positions the firm at the forefront of the industry's evolving landscape.

After:

> The company's patents cover technologies including quantum computing and sustainable energy.

The technological scope is available in the input. Patent counts and grant
dates would require additional material.

## Example 4: Research description

Before:

> This study serves as a comprehensive examination of the interplay between social media usage and adolescent mental health outcomes. Highlighting the intricate relationship between online engagement patterns and psychological wellbeing, the research underscores the crucial role of parental mediation in mitigating potential adverse effects.

After:

> The study examines the relationship between social media use and adolescent mental health, including the role of parental mediation in limiting potential adverse effects.

The revision preserves the subject and stated research focus. The input gives
no sample size, study design, measured association, or treatment effect, so
the revised text makes none of those claims.

## Example 5: Product launch

Before:

> The launch represents a significant milestone in the company's journey toward excellence. Moreover, it showcases the organization's unwavering dedication to delivering products that epitomize quality and innovation. Industry observers have noted that this release could potentially reshape the competitive landscape.

After:

> Industry observers say the product launch could change competition in the market.

The uncertainty and attribution remain. The source does not identify those
observers; an actual publication may need that attribution resolved. Price,
launch date, countries, and preorder figures cannot be inferred from this text.

## Example 6: General English with an informational voice

Before:

> AI-assisted coding is spreading from hobbyist experiments to enterprise rollouts, from solo developers to cross-functional teams. The tools can generate documentation, tests, and refactors. Their effect on productivity remains uncertain. Known problems include hallucinations, bias, and accountability.

After:

> AI coding assistants are spreading from hobbyist experiments to enterprise use, among both individual developers and teams. They can generate documentation, tests, and refactors. Their effect on productivity remains uncertain, and problems with hallucinations, bias, and accountability remain.

The informational voice stays informational. Sentence flow can change without
adding the author's personal adoption plans, enthusiasm, or reservations.

## Example 7: A personal voice already present

Before:

> The library added 50,000 books last year. Membership grew by 12%. I keep going back because it's the only quiet place in my neighborhood.

After:

> The library added 50,000 books last year, and membership grew by 12%. I keep going back because it's the only quiet place in my neighborhood.

The facts and the first-person reason are supplied. Either sentence rhythm
can work; a stylistic pass need not replace wording that already reads well.

## How to use the examples

Identify the actual information and voice before choosing an edit. Compare the
result with the input: what fact, relationship, uncertainty, or personal stance
changed? Judge whether those changes were authorized. Greater specificity is
useful only when the material supports it; shorter text is useful only when
the reader retains the information they need.
